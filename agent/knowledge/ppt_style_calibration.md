# PPT Style Calibration

Applies to visual direction, image-page production and visual review. Style
choices are conditional on the brief; no palette or illustrative tradition in
this card is the default for every RCA deck.

## A Working Style Reference

Use current accepted examples across the page roles that matter. Explain what
to preserve: relative color intensity, background temperature, stroke and edge
quality, midtones, transparent layers, material, depth, lettering and spacing.
A named tradition or adjective alone is insufficient. Chinese ink style, for
example, comes from ink modeling, wash, paper and spatial treatment; adding
mountains or bamboo cannot repair unrelated flat vector illustration.

Label each reference by allowed borrowing: content baseline, style, anatomy,
verified evidence/asset or neighboring continuity. Style references contribute
mark-making and material, not sample sizes, labels, claims or organs. A correct
CT/MRI label does not prove correct anatomy or view. Use an explicit anatomical
reference for a medical object when its identity is at risk.

English, Chinese, Arabic/Roman numerals and publication labels all participate
in the lettering style. A paper texture behind rigid printed lettering does
not satisfy a hand-drawn brief. Preserve readable scientific notation and names.

## Calibrate Before Scaling

Choose a small, risk-driven set that tests different roles or loads, such as a
mechanism, result and narrative pivot. Produce and read those pages, compare
with the accepted examples and new neighbors, then continue automatically within
the authorized scope. Already accepted current pages can provide this evidence;
do not add an approval checkpoint or repeatedly regenerate a sample ritual.

Keep one illustrator's palette, lettering, material and space while allowing
different compositions. Consistency does not mean repeated title positions,
card counts, layouts, banners or density. Let evidence and page role determine
complexity; assess both overload and missing explanation. Improve grouping and
composition before discarding necessary proof for whitespace.

RCA has a selectable profile catalog at
`prompts/ppt_deck/style_profiles.json`. The current built-in profiles are
`rca_handdrawn_lecture`, `rca_chinese_ink_lecture`,
`rca_alariko_editorial_illustration`, and
`rca_ukiyo_e_woodblock_lecture`, and `rca_warm_animation_lecture`. A profile is a visual contract, not a story
template: it may define palette, lettering, material, composition tendencies,
prompt language, forbidden regressions and review checks, but it cannot change
the claim spine, page duty, facts, source language or authoring-lane admission.

For a new profile, the reliable sequence is:

1. Select the profile by id and bind its current catalog revision to the visual
   direction output.
2. Define a written visual recipe and test representative page roles, or reuse
   current accepted pages as review evidence. Supplying an image anchor is
   optional; an accepted review example is not automatically a generation input.
3. Write the page-local first-glance object, relationship encoding, visible-copy
   budget and evidence whitelist before composing the image prompt.
4. Generate a complete 16:9 lecture page with the style and explanation designed
   together. Do not generate a decorative scene and then search for places to
   put the title and labels.
5. Review in this order: lecture structure and source fidelity, readability and
   relationships, cross-page continuity, then similarity to the style anchor.

The Alariko-inspired profile is a project/owner-selected option. Its reusable
traits are layered paper, irregular contours, blue-green and warm ochre planes,
local perspective, translucent washes, directional shadows and fine pen
lettering. A full room, window view, mountain, bamboo or random prop is not a
style requirement; add an environment only when it explains the page.

The Chinese ink profile follows the same boundary. Ink style comes from ink
modeling, wash, paper, dry-brush edges, spatial restraint and lettering. Adding
mountains, bamboo or seals without semantic work is a forbidden regression.

The ukiyo-e profile uses woodblock contours, limited indigo/vermilion/ochre
planes, rice paper and asymmetric framing. Random Japanese text, decorative
waves and cultural motifs cannot substitute for a visible lecture relationship.

## Relative Review And Repair

Review the changed page individually, old versus new, then in its neighborhood.
Check whether it became flatter, rougher, heavier, more crowded or less legible.
Compare actual palette relationships and object modeling, not style-name tags.
Newly accepted stronger pages may raise the benchmark; older pages are not an
aesthetic ceiling. Latest user acceptance determines which benchmark applies.

A redraw can preserve the words and change their meaning through the objects:
exchangeable AI modules and a microscope are not interchangeable metaphors.
Specify unfamiliar object semantics before generation. After a local correction,
inspect the whole affected page for drifting numbers, labels, arrows and anatomy.
Repeated near-misses call for a corrected content/object brief or new composition,
not endless editing of the wrong image. Generalize the failure and correction;
do not turn a user's particular taste into a global style rule.

## Route-Specific Prompts And Optional Image Inputs

Calibrate the actual selected image route. A useful GPT-image prompt or image
anchor does not establish that another route needs the same input strategy.
When the user selects a text-only route, start with a coherent description of
medium, contours, color relationships, light, object treatment and the scene.
A reference image is a conditional tool, not a quality prerequisite: it can
also import an unwanted composition, generic visual language or old lettering.
Add one only for a concrete need such as anatomy, identity, a verified asset
or demonstrated continuity, and label its permitted role.

A warm hand-drawn animation brief may intentionally use clean contours,
clear painted planes, simplified characters, cream highlights and teal-green
shadows. Do not automatically downgrade cleanliness or force rough ink and
paper texture. Use the latest user-approved result to resolve style words.
Keep an earlier Alariko/editorial option separate when its accepted rendering
has a different material treatment; do not silently rewrite existing decks.

Separate art calibration from lecture production. A beautiful scene proves a
visual vocabulary, not a readable lecture page. Transfer the selected craft to
the page's explanatory objects, relationships, evidence and lettering. Keep
the exact scientific copy without burying the style under a repeated list of
unrelated negatives. Review anatomy, arrows, captions and citations again.

For an independent production comparison, keep corresponding finished pages
out of generation inputs. Use source text and the declared director brief;
record any independent assets, actual prompts, image-input count and attempts.
A matched lecture task with adapted prompts is a workflow comparison, not a
controlled claim that one model is intrinsically better. Reference-conditioned
reconstruction is a separate test and cannot establish independent generation.
Keep first-pass quality evidence distinct from the delivery goal. A request
for from-scratch authoring does not itself impose a one-attempt hard stop.
Continue authorized prompt/recipe improvement and independent regeneration
until usable; do not call a later selection the first pass. Honor a numeric
experiment-only call limit only within its actual scope. Defects inform the
next initial recipe rather than ending an authorized usable-delivery task.

## Mixed-Object Style Stability

A shared style prefix does not establish that a heterogeneous medical page
will use one illustrator's craft. Bind the selected visual language separately
to characters, anatomy diagrams, dossiers/tools, relationships and lettering.
Detailed medical nouns may elicit conventional realistic anatomy or photo-like
textures even when the global prompt asks for warm animation. Preserve anatomy,
modality and plane while specifying the diagram's actual marks and painted
planes. Verified real medical assets remain evidence with their own role; do
not fabricate or stylize them merely to satisfy uniformity.

Compare those object treatments with a currently accepted page, without needing
to send that page as model input. A passing simple scene is not evidence that
multimodal evidence or a feedback diagram is stable. Validate different page
roles with the same frozen recipe and declared one-pass budget before claiming
first-pass stability. A rejected output remains a rejected output; labels,
profile completeness and file packaging cannot promote it. Record a failed object/style binding as a process finding and improve the
initial recipe. When the user wants a usable result, continue authorized
from-scratch generations; never confuse that work with an experiment-only
measurement or conceal how many generations were needed.

## From-Scratch Production Still Ends In A Usable Artifact

Separate invocation strategy, evaluation evidence and completion. From-scratch
means a page is authored from source text/director intent, without conditioning
on a corresponding finished page. It does not mean that a failed first output
completes the task. Improve the production brief and repeat independent
authoring within the user's latest scope, preserve accepted pages, review the
whole selected output and complete packaging once the result is usable.

When ordinary generated imagery repeatedly invents a medical modality or
anatomical site, stop treating labels as fidelity. Use verified scientific
assets as declared evidence layers when the brief supports them; reserve their
space in the page plan. Preserve original evidence and provenance. This is
planned verified-asset composition, not a text/visual cover-up for an ordinary
content error. Reference-image policy for aesthetic calibration is independent
from the need to preserve true medical evidence.

For scientific copy, a verified standard journal abbreviation may reduce
lettering errors when the brief permits it; retain the complete title, year
and DOI in notes. Inspect names, modality/site, cohort-to-value bindings and
arrows in the selected pixels. OCR disagreements are review prompts, not
automatic acceptance or rejection. A small prompt change can also alter
lettering, paper temperature and object rendering elsewhere on the page;
review the entire new output and its accepted neighbors after each selection.

Report usable-output effort separately from generation latency: include all
known calls, asset preparation and review scope. An accepted selection reached
through independent regenerations establishes a working production path; it
does not establish a high first-pass success rate. Preserve accepted page
bytes and package verified medical assets with reconstructable placement or
independent layers rather than repainting evidence for stylistic uniformity.

## H3 Full-Slide Text Route

When the selected image route requires the workbench's from-scratch, full-slide
text generation, keep the route intact: H3 produces each complete 16:9 page
with lettering and relationships. Do not silently substitute illustration-only
assets plus HTML/PPT text composition. Use one byte-stable `shared_style_prefix`
for the entire batch and a structured page payload with title, first glance,
reading order, objects, exact copy, evidence and avoid items. The prefix owns
paper temperature, ink, lettering, line weight, shadows, background decoration,
and the lecture-PPT guardrail; the payload owns page facts and relationships.

Submit the pages with the same route, aspect ratio, area and n. Exclude
corresponding finished pages and prior H3 pages from generation inputs. A real
paper image may be supplied as a declared scientific-evidence input, distinct
from a style anchor. Review all pages together for background, lettering,
palette, title hierarchy and reading order before accepting any one page. If
drift appears, revise the shared prefix or page-family rule and regenerate the
affected pages from scratch; do not append page-local style words, use filters,
cover text, or turn one finished page into another page's reference. H3's
current image connector exposes no stable cross-request seed, so this route
can stabilize the visible system and process while remaining honest about
pixel-level nondeterminism.

If a same-prefix batch drifts, review the actual shared rendering behavior:
character simplification, contour emphasis, color-plane/light relationships,
medical-object treatment, lettering and paper. An identical prefix is input
evidence, not a visual verdict. A style-only material/lettering anchor can be
calibrated only when the selected route and current user constraints permit
image inputs; it must contain no page facts or corresponding finished layout.
For an enhancement-disabled H3 API brief, remain on text-to-image: an image
input selects a different endpoint whose enhancement behavior is not equivalent.

### Provider direct-API route

When a provider-hosted coding channel is observed to rewrite image prompts and
the user requests the provider's direct API, route the selected image backend
through the provider-owned CLI/API rather than continuing to tune the rewriting
host. For MiniMax H3 Image, record the region, model, size, prompt-enhancement
flag, reference count, response id, usage, and whether `revised_prompts` equals
the submitted prompt. The pure text-to-image route can disable enhancement;
The current official H3 CLI routes `--image` to `/v1/images/edits` and locally
rejects combining it with `--no-prompt-enhance`, stating that edit enhancement
is always on. When the brief requires enhancement disabled, keep the no-image
`/v1/images/generations` route; do not silently change that constraint. A direct API success
proves transport and prompt ownership, not visual readiness; review the full
page and preserve scientific evidence rules separately.

### Reusable direct-API and rendering baseline

For the user-selected H3 route, preserve provider-owned authentication and pass
prompt text as an argument value, never executable shell code. Pin the actual
model (`MiniMax-H3-Image`), region, explicit size, output format and `n`; use
`--no-prompt-enhance` for faithful text-to-image. Retain the full submitted
prompt, separate prefix/payload hashes, provider revision, response identity,
usage, elapsed time and output hash. A matching `revised_prompts` field proves
prompt fidelity; an absent field remains unverified. Do not infer H3 seed
support from generic CLI help. Reconcile unknown generation/download outcomes
before another non-idempotent call.

Freeze a reusable recipe from the **selected image's submitted request** and
verify its hashes. The last-edited prompt can belong to a rejected attempt.
Keep an accepted recipe separate from the mutable experiment directory, with
an explicit user-acceptance scope and known artistic/content limitations.
Provider transport, prompt fidelity, cross-page coherence, artistic richness,
fact validity and release acceptance remain distinct judgments. Preserve the
user's accepted coherence baseline while improving art; do not promote it to
pixel reproduction or guaranteed first-pass success.

For a selected warm animation style, use adult two-dimensional characters,
selective light/heavy or omitted contours, cream-lit planes, cool blue/teal
shadows and simplified matte painted masses. Share the same abstraction level
across people, medical diagrams, dossiers and tools. Repeated demands for
individual hair, dense coat folds, thick page edges, precise keyboard keys and
uniform dark closed contours can pull H3 toward stiff realistic illustration.
Richness can instead come from meaningful gesture, overlaps, lighting and
color-plane rhythm. This is an optional rendering recipe, not an RCA-wide art
default and not evidence of reproducing a named illustrator.

Chinese handwriting requires actual inspection of title, labels, conclusion,
Latin terms and numbers; specify one fine-pen running/regular hand with natural
entry/exit, small joins and spacing/baseline variation. English success cannot
stand in for Chinese acceptance. Keep page payloads about facts, objects,
locations and relationships; avoid page-local rendering/lettering overrides.
After a shared art change, review representative roles together. Change one
art factor group at a time against the accepted baseline and retain both the
first output and selected repair identity.

If generated scientific images repeatedly confuse modality or anatomy, use
verified original evidence or an accurate record/method illustration suitable
for the page's duty, disclose that expression change and preserve the study
facts. Do not use stylistic coherence to justify invented clinical scans.
